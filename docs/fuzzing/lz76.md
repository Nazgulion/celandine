# LZ76 fuzz campaign

On 2026-10-23, the new LZ76 target passed **100,000 executions** in six reported
seconds without sanitizer failures or mathematical assertion failures. Final
counters were `cov=130`, `ft=267`, and RSS 62 MiB. These are instrumented coverage
features, not line-coverage percentages; RSS includes the harness and sanitizer.

```sh
python3 scripts/fuzz_campaign.py --target lz76 --runs 100000
```

The run used Linux x86-64, nightly-2025-10-25, cargo-fuzz 0.13.1,
libfuzzer-sys 0.4.10, AddressSanitizer, debug assertions, and overflow checks.
Settings were seed 20260920, one worker, maximum length 128 bytes, ten-second
per-input timeout, and a 2048 MiB RSS limit. The input is raw bytes without a
header; bytes beyond the limit are ignored by the harness. The limit bounds
the scalar algorithm and independent substring-search oracle costs.

The oracle grows candidate phrases until they are absent before their final
byte, independently of the library's longest-match source scan. Checks use exact
integer equality, bounds, bijective XOR renaming, and one-symbol extension.
Fourteen retained seeds cover empty/single input, overlap, periodicity, distinct
labels, arbitrary bytes, two published examples, late novelty, and reversal
sensitivity. Stable replay now covers 119 generated seeds plus one retained TV
oracle regression across all seven targets. Empty corpus files are replayed by
stable tests even when libFuzzer omits them during corpus initialization.

the process, with sanitizers enabled. CI automatically includes this seventh
target in its bounded 10,000-execution smoke campaigns. Counts include corpus
initialization and are not distinct-input counts; passing is bounded evidence,
not exhaustive verification or a replacement for the independent history tests.
